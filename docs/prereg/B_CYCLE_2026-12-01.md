**What a failure looks like** (negative controls, scenarios `c1_activity_missing`,
`c3_extra_broker_order`, `c4_transition_without_event`): an absent OPASN / OPEXP for the call
prints `broker_type=- ... flags=TRANSITION_ONLY`; an order at the broker the journal does not
hold prints `journal_status=- ... flags=BROKER_ONLY`; a journal transition no broker event
explains prints `ACTIVITY symbol=- ... flags=TRANSITION_ONLY`. Each makes
`discrepancies=1`, exit 3 (journal_report.py:589).

**Known blind spot:** the report does not check the sign or size of the OPTRD share trade it
pairs (journal_report.py:459-468). A call assignment whose share trade shows `+100` still
prints `flags=OK` and `discrepancies=0` (findings doc, F1). Baxman checks item 2's
`share_qty=-100` by eye until that is fixed.

Paper non-trade activities lag about a day (docs/INSTRUCTIONS.md:238); if the call's
OPASN / OPEXP has not posted when the report runs, the 1 Dec transition prints
`TRANSITION_ONLY`. Re-run the report the next day before calling it a failure.
